- **Browser Storage & Cookies:** Secure session identifiers, shopping cart tokens, user preference settings, and Google Analytics 4 performance metrics.

## 2. Cookie Management & Browser Storage

Home Rituals adheres to global privacy benchmarks including GDPR and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. We categorize our cookies and browser storage into three distinct categories:

### A. Strictly Essential Cookies (Always Active)
These technical cookies and storage mechanisms are strictly necessary for core platform operation and security:
- **Authentication Session (`home_rituals_refresh`):** A hardened, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` cookie used to securely maintain user logins and execute automatic refresh token rotation without exposing credentials to client-side scripts.
- **CSRF Defense:** Custom origin verification headers and session tokens that protect your account against cross-site request forgery attacks.
- **Shopping Cart Persistence:** Client-side local storage that preserves guest cart selections across page reloads and browser restarts until merged securely upon sign-in.
*Because these cookies are essential to provide you with the services requested, they cannot be switched off.*

### B. Analytics & Performance Cookies (Consent-Controlled)
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Aggregates anonymous metrics regarding site traffic, product discovery paths, and page performance.
- **Consent Mode v2:** Analytics storage defaults to **denied** until you explicitly choose "Accept All" or enable Analytics in our Cookie Settings. If consent is declined, no analytics cookies are read or stored.

### C. Marketing & Targeting Cookies (Consent-Controlled)
- Used to evaluate campaign effectiveness and deliver relevant curations without intrusive advertising. Defaults to **denied** until affirmative consent is provided.

## 3. How to Manage or Withdraw Cookie Consent
You retain full control over your cookie choices at all times:
- **Interactive Preferences:** You can update your choices or withdraw consent at any time by clicking **"Cookie Settings"** in the footer of any page on our website.
- **Browser Controls:** You may also configure your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block or delete cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact checkout and account login features.
